08 / SCOPE & NEXT WORK

A prototype with
clear boundaries.

The project makes workflow behavior inspectable and testable with synthetic data; it does not claim production readiness or retrieval quality.

  • Schema-validated extraction
  • Replay protection and review gate
  • Isolated mock browser scenarios
  • Trace instrumentation
↗OPEN EVALUATION WORK

Questions still to test

  • Retrieval quality against a judged set
  • Provider quality across representative inputs
  • Operational resilience at realistic volume
  • Deployment access control and runbook review

Reviewable automation should make it clear what it knows, what it cannot validate, and where a person decides.
